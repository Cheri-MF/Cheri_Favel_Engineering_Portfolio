PROJECT 02 · ELECTROCHEMISTRY

Zinc electrolysis trials

Planned, set up and ran zinc electrolysis trials with plate electrodes from start to finish: experimental design, sampling, analysis and the final technical report.

ELECTROCHEMISTRY · END-TO-END OWNERSHIP

PROJECT 03 · REACTOR DEVELOPMENT

Continuous rotating-cathode electrowinning

Ran hands-on copper and cobalt trials on a continuous rotating-cathode electrowinning unit, studying deposit form, scraping and release, and contributed to a scale-up proposal for zinc recovery from tailings water.

ELECTROWINNING · SCALE-UP CONSIDERATIONS

PROJECT 04 · REACTOR TESTING

Gold recovery from refinery eluate

Ran laboratory-scale electrochemical reactor trials to recover residual gold from refinery eluate. Documented the experimental setup, sampling and analytical work, and drew the sample-processing flow diagrams.

Manganese recovery & processing

Reviewed and revised the design basis and process description, and drew PFDs, for a manganese sulphate monohydrate demonstration plant. Planned and ran selective-precipitation investigations for manganese fines, with preliminary electrolysis screening.
