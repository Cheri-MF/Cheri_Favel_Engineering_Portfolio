02 / SELECTED WORK

Selected engineering work.

Selected projects spanning process engineering, reactor development, industrial water treatment and resource recovery.

01 / ELECTROCHEMISTRY

PROJECT 01 · ELECTROCHEMISTRY

Cobalt electrowinning

Wrote the cobalt-processing literature review, planned the test matrix, identified and corrected an over-dilute stock electrolyte, and ran 25+ parallel-plate tests varying concentration, temperature and pH to define the operating window. Then ran rotating-cathode trials (cobalt and copper) and identified deposit release as a key design constraint.

ELECTROWINNING · TEST DESIGN · OPERATING WINDOW

PROJECT 02 · REACTOR DESIGN

Gold recovery from refinery eluate

Ran laboratory electrochemical reactor trials (current density, voltage, flow rate, residence time), supported by ICP-OES/XRF data analysis. Then designed the next-phase custom sludging reactor (conical acrylic shell, concentric electrodes) and authored its process description, mechanical PFD and preliminary mass balance for five parallel reactors.

REACTOR DESIGN · ELECTROCHEMISTRY · MASS BALANCE

PROJECT 03 · ELECTROCHEMISTRY

Zinc electrolysis trials

Planned, set up, ran and reported plate-electrode zinc electrolysis trials end to end, including experimental design, sampling, analysis and recommendations.

ZINC ELECTROLYSIS · EXPERIMENTAL DESIGN

PROJECT 04 · PROCESS DESIGN

Manganese sulphate monohydrate plant

Drew and reviewed PFDs and P&IDs, and reviewed and revised the design basis and process description, for a manganese sulphate monohydrate demonstration plant. Ran selective-precipitation investigations for manganese fines.

PFDs/P&IDs · DESIGN BASIS · SELECTIVE PRECIPITATION

PROJECT 05 · INDUSTRIAL WASTEWATER

Mine-tailings water treatment

Investigated metal removal from synthetic mine-tailings water using oxidation and selective precipitation.

Research proposal contribution

Wrote the laboratory-trial section of a research proposal for iron, zinc and manganese recovery from Peruvian tailings water.

Scope of the zinc proposal

Contributed to a proposal for zinc recovery from tailings water, including process development and scale-up considerations.

OXIDATION · SELECTIVE PRECIPITATION · RESEARCH PROPOSAL

PROJECT 06 · RARE EARTHS

Rare-earth precipitation & plant economics

Ran lanthanide precipitation experiments and contributed calculations to a CAPEX/OPEX model for a neodymium metal plant.

LANTHANIDE PRECIPITATION · CAPEX/OPEX

PROJECT 07 · PUBLISHED RESEARCH

Phlogopite leaching kinetics

Designed nitric-acid leaching experiments and characterised mineral feed and residues. Identified diffusion-controlled kinetics using a one-dimensional D1 model and determined activation energies. Published in Clays and Clay Minerals (2022).

LEACHING · D1 KINETIC MODEL · ACTIVATION ENERGIES

Other projects

Other work included Fe/Al precipitation investigations, a Ni-MH battery-recycling literature review, techno-economic evaluation of neodymium metal production, a galvanising-process review and a seawater electrolysis literature review.

My academic work also included a portable membrane-based water purification concept.
